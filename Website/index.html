What's in it

Built for avatars that have to look right in worlds you didn't light.

World lighting

LTCGI area lights and VRC Light Volumes, both with specular, when those packages are present. Falls back to Unity probes otherwise.

AudioLink

Emission, geometry break, and glitch driven by the music. Idles quietly in worlds with no AudioLink object.

Toon and PBR

Ramp shading with metallic, smoothness and AO from a packed map, plus subsurface scattering for skin and thin cloth.

Effects

Interior mapping, refraction, outlines, matcaps, iridescence, dissolve, and a screen shader for panels and displays.

Debug views

Render one term on its own — albedo, AO, ambient, reflection — instead of guessing which input turned a material black.

Its own inspector

Material UI, locking, animated-property tracking, and a channel packer. No ThryEditor required.
